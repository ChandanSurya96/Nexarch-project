# User Personas

**Purpose:** Who Nexarch is building for. These personas ground the acceptance criteria in [product-requirements.md](../product-requirements.md) and the flow in [user-journey.md](./user-journey.md) in real people rather than an abstract "user." Treat these as working hypotheses to validate with real interviews once there are real users, not settled fact.

---

## Anaya, 26 — The Overwhelmed New Investor

**Situation:** Started investing about 18 months ago through a discount broker after seeing friends do SIPs. Follows several finance creators on Instagram and YouTube. Has made a few impulsive trades based on "hot tip" reels she now regrets.

**Goals:** Learn what a *good* portfolio actually looks like, from real examples rather than curated highlight reels. Wants to build conviction in her own approach rather than chase whatever's trending.

**Frustrations:** Can't tell which creators are actually good investors versus good at making content. Every "portfolio reveal" she sees online could be cherry-picked or fabricated, and she has no way to check.

**How Nexarch helps:** The Public Investor Library gives her verifiable examples to study even before she trusts any individual community member. Discovery filters (Long-term, Value, Dividend) let her find portfolios that match a style she's trying to learn, not just whoever's loudest.

## Rohan, 34 — The Experienced DIY Investor

**Situation:** IT professional, has managed his own portfolio for 8 years with a disciplined long-term approach. Occasionally shares views in a WhatsApp group of coworkers who ask for his opinion, but has no way to demonstrate his actual track record beyond talking about it.

**Goals:** Build a credible, checkable record of his investing approach. Open to eventually monetizing that credibility (Phase 4) but doesn't want to become a "finfluencer" performing confidence for engagement.

**Frustrations:** Existing "share your portfolio" options online (screenshots, forum posts) are unverifiable and easy to fake, which makes his genuine track record look no different from someone making numbers up.

**How Nexarch helps:** A verified badge gives his real portfolio an evidentiary weight that a screenshot never could. He doesn't need a following to get value — the profile itself is the artifact.

## Priya, 41 — The Cautious First-Time Equity Investor

**Situation:** Has saved conservatively for years (FDs, insurance-linked products) and is considering equities for the first time, prompted by inflation eroding her fixed-income returns. Deeply wary of scams after hearing about SEBI's crackdowns on finfluencer schemes in the news.

**Goals:** Find low-risk, long-term examples to model her first steps after, from people who are demonstrably not selling her anything.

**Frustrations:** Everything she's found online so far is trying to sell a course, a subscription, or a "strategy" — she has no neutral reference point.

**How Nexarch helps:** The Low-risk Investors and Long-term Investors discovery filters, combined with clearly-labeled Public Investor profiles that aren't selling anything, give her a lower-stakes way to build intuition before committing real capital to a strategy of her own.

## Vikram, 29 — The Aspiring Finance Creator

**Situation:** Has been posting market commentary for a year, building a small but engaged following. Increasingly aware that SEBI's 2024–2026 finfluencer enforcement has raised the bar for what he can legally say and monetize without registration.

**Goals:** Differentiate his credibility with evidence rather than confidence — and eventually monetize legitimately, in a way that doesn't put him in the same regulatory position as the finfluencers SEBI has been acting against.

**Frustrations:** He's a good investor but a smaller name than the accounts that just talk convincingly; there's no way for his actual results to speak for him.

**How Nexarch helps:** Verification lets his portfolio, not his follower count, do the persuading. Phase 4's Creator Economy is directly built for someone like him — with the explicit caveat (see [monetization.md](./monetization.md)) that monetizing "research" or "insights" likely requires the same SEBI registration path other finfluencers have had to pursue.
